# Brand rules

- Palette: near-black #0b0b0d, charcoal #16171b, gunmetal #2a2d33, brass/gold #c9a227, signal amber #ffb000, one cold blue #5ec8ff (reserved for "the brain").
- Captions: amber text, black outline, bottom-centre, never covering faces, max ~6 words per line.
- Thumbnails: dark, high contrast, gold Art Deco frame, title in caps, max 6 words.
- Only original or licensed assets. No third-party logos, characters, franchise names or trademarks in visuals.
- Video: vertical 9:16 for shorts, 45-60 s, captions burned in.
- Every claim on screen must have passed the fact-checker.
