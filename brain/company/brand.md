# Brand rules

- Palette (for what WE add: captions, titles, thumbnails, frames): near-black #0b0b0d, charcoal #16171b, gunmetal #2a2d33, brass/gold #c9a227, signal amber #ffb000, one cold blue #5ec8ff (reserved for "the brain"). The creator's raw footage keeps its own colours.
- Captions: amber text, black outline, bottom-centre, never covering faces, max ~6 words per cue. They must match what the speaker says (fix obvious transcription errors only).
- Thumbnails: 1280x720 (platform standard, also used for vertical shorts), dark and high contrast, gold Art Deco frame, title in caps, max 6 words, made from a frame of the clip.
- Video: vertical 9:16 (1080x1920) for shorts, max 60 s, captions burned in.
- Only original or licensed assets. No third-party logos, characters, franchise names or trademarks in anything we add.
- Text WE write on screen (titles, thumbnail text, overlays) must be backed by the source. The speaker's own words in captions are theirs; flag anything risky for the owner rather than failing the clip.
